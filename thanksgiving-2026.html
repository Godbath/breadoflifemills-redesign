Our First Thanksgiving

A Celebration of Gratitude, Community & Giving

What an incredible welcome we've received from the Sierra Vista community!

First, we'd like to apologize for not announcing our arrival at the Sierra Vista Farmers Market sooner. We've been operating at full capacity preparing for our debut, and between milling fresh flour, preparing our sourdough, and getting everything ready for market day, time got away from us!

But we're thrilled to finally announce that Bread of Life Mills has joined the Sierra Vista Farmers Market as a permanent weekly vendor!

We want to extend our sincere gratitude to the Sierra Vista Farmers Market for welcoming us and providing local producers like ourselves with an opportunity to share the fruits of our labor with the community. We're equally grateful to everyone who stopped by our booth, asked questions, sampled our products, and shared their enthusiasm for what we're doing.

That enthusiasm means more to us than you might realize. It's the encouragement and genuine interest of people like you that continually renew our passion for bringing wholesome, freshly milled grains and traditionally fermented sourdough bread back to our tables.

We'd also like to extend a special thank you to CoolFM for stopping by and giving us an opportunity to introduce Bread of Life Mills and share a little about our products with their audience!

Our First Thanksgiving: A Special Holiday Thank You!

It's hard to believe that Bread of Life Mills was born just ten months ago, and here we are, preparing to celebrate our very first Thanksgiving as a family business!

Considering how much has happened in these ten months, and how warmly the Sierra Vista community has welcomed us, we couldn't think of a better way to celebrate than by inviting you to share in our first Thanksgiving with your own families.

Sharing Our Blessings with Our Neighbors

Thanksgiving is about more than the abundance on our own tables. It's also an opportunity to remember our neighbors who may be struggling to put food on theirs.

In that spirit, for every Thanksgiving preorder loaf sold, Bread of Life Mills will donate $1 to our local Society of St. Vincent de Paul's food bank, as well as 100% of all gratuities received during this promotion, helping support its mission of providing food and assistance to families in need.

We believe that the blessings we've received are meant to be shared, and we're grateful for the opportunity to make this small contribution alongside the community that has so generously welcomed us.
